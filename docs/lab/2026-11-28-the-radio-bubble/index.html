Drag the slider forward. Every year you move the clock, the bubble of our broadcasts pushes one light-year deeper into space. Gold stars are ones the news of us has already reached; dim ones still haven't heard a thing.

Broadcasting began around 1895 (Marconi's spark-gap experiments); the deliberate Arecibo Message to the stars followed in 1974. Sirius, 8.6 light-years away, and Vega, 25, have already "received" decades of Earth's history; the strongest Arecibo-era radar could in principle be detected from as far as ~12,000 light-years — but the galaxy is ~100,000 light-years across.
