Real orbital mechanics, computed in your browser. Where every planet is today — or on the day you were born.

Top-down view of the ecliptic plane. Distances use a √ scale so the inner planets stay visible; the Sun is the bright dot at center. Click a planet below to find it.
Positions computed from NASA JPL's approximate Keplerian elements (Standish & Williams 1992, valid 1800–2050). Accurate to roughly a few hundredths of a degree for the inner planets, less for the outer ones — plenty for "which constellation," not for navigation. The constellation shown is each planet's geocentric position (where you'd see it in the sky from Earth, ignoring light-travel time). The Sun's sign is its apparent position from Earth — the astronomical version of the horoscope's "your sign."
