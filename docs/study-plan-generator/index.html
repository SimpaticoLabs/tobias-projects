🧠 Study Tips for Better Results

🍅 Pomodoro Technique
Study in 25-minute focused blocks with 5-minute breaks. After 4 blocks, take a longer 15-30min break. Great for maintaining focus across multiple subjects.
🔁 Active Recall
Instead of re-reading notes, close the book and recall key concepts from memory. Use flashcards, teach someone else, or write everything you remember about a topic.
⏳ Spaced Repetition
Review material at increasing intervals: 1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 2 weeks. Your study plan already schedules review sessions — stick to them!
📝 Practice Testing
Take practice exams under timed conditions. This reduces test anxiety and reveals weak spots. Your plan includes practice phases — make them count.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make a study plan for my exams?

Enter your exam date, the subjects/topics to cover, and the hours you can study — the generator builds a day-by-day schedule that spreads the work up to the exam. Free, no sign-up.

Is this study plan generator free?

Yes — completely free, no sign-up, no ads, and it works on mobile.

How many hours a day should I study before an exam?

It depends on your exam date and workload; the generator distributes your available hours across the days remaining so you cover everything without last-minute cramming.

Can I plan multiple subjects at once?

Yes — add each subject or topic and the plan interleaves them across your study days.
