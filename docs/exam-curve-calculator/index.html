How each curve method works
  • Flat: add the same points to every score, capped at the exam max. Simple and transparent — good when most students missed the same tricky section.
  • Scale top to 100%: multiply every score so the highest raw score becomes 100%. Preserves the rank order and the gaps between students; a strong top score pulls everyone's scores up proportionally.
  • Square-root curve: new = √(raw / max) × max. Raises low scores the most and high scores the least — the classic "everyone gets a bump, weaker students get the biggest" curve.
