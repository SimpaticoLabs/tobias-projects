Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate what I need on my final exam?

Enter your current grade, your target grade, and the final exam's weight (from your syllabus). The calculator returns the exact score you need on the final to reach your target - instantly, free, no sign-up.

What grade do I need on my final to get an A or B?

It depends on your current grade and the final's weight. Enter both plus your target (e.g. 90% for an A) and the tool shows the exact final-exam score required - or tells you if your target is already locked in.

Is this final grade calculator free?

Yes - completely free, no sign-up, no ads, and it works offline once the page loads.

How is a weighted grade calculated?

Each assignment contributes its grade times its weight; the weighted grade is the sum of those contributions divided by total weight. The weighted-grade mode does this automatically as you add items.
