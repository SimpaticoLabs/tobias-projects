Frequently asked questions

What is this Polymarket scanner?

A free, no-sign-up tool that pulls live markets from Polymarket's public API and lets you sort them by trading volume, liquidity, or how soon they close, filter by keyword, and see each market's implied probability at a glance.

Does it need a wallet or sign-up?

No. It's read-only and reads Polymarket's public data directly in your browser — no wallet, no account, no sign-up. It can't place trades; for that you use Polymarket itself (each market links out).

What does implied probability mean?

A market's price for an outcome (0–1) is the crowd's implied probability. A "Yes" at 0.63 implies a 63% chance. The scanner shows the leading outcome's implied probability for each market.

Is the data live?

Yes — it fetches current markets from Polymarket's Gamma API each time you load or refresh. Prices and volume are as current as Polymarket's public API.
